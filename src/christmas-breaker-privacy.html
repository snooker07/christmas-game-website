Who is responsible for the app?

Christmas Breaker is published by Benjamin Fundelits. You can reach us at support@fundelits.com.

What stays on your device?

The game does not require a Fundelits account. It stores your best score, audio settings and a local record of your Remove Ads entitlement on your device. Removing the app can clear these local settings. Your Apple purchase can be restored using Restore in the main menu.

Advertising and tracking

The app uses Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and the Unity Ads adapter to serve ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. The SDKs may process information such as IP address, device and app information, advertising identifiers where available, and ad interactions. See Unity’s privacy policy for its data uses and choices.

On iPhone and iPad, Apple’s tracking permission controls whether the app can track you across other companies’ apps and websites. You can change it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ads may still appear if you decline tracking. Remove Ads is optional; rewarded ads are a separate feature.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Remove Ads purchase. The game uses the purchase result to unlock the entitlement and does not receive your card or bank details. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Support and deletion requests

If you email us, we use your email address and the information you provide to answer and follow up. We retain correspondence while it is needed for those purposes or as required by law. Contact us to ask about your data or request deletion. Unity and Apple handle their records under their respective policies.
